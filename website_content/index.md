## Welcome to Ashwood

Ashwood is a Worldbuilding & Civ server that allows for open conflicts and PvP.
## What You'll Find Here

**📜 Worldbuilding & Lore** - Explore deep lore and worldbuilding from existing groups, or create your own. We have no set era or lore, so it is an open book.

**⚔️ PvP & Raiding** - If you aren't in a safe town, you are at risk of getting killed if you aren't careful. There could be highway robbers in the wilderness, or someone turning on you during travels. Even towns aren't safe, during the weekends, anybody is able to raid you, so strong walls or a good militia is important.
