We want to provide a long lasting world where players have complete agency over what happens and what they choose to do. A lot of vintage story servers have tedious whitelist proccess and a strict set of rules on the RP itself, often making story telling a tedious proccess, and conflict very rare. We believe that allowing players to have more agency is a more productive way to tell an overarching story. Not everyone is going to be nice and spare you on the road, and not all people want to live peacefully. How do you live with that and deal with them as time moves on? Worldbuilding doesn't happen just through RP interactions that won't be recorded, but the breathing and changing world and how players interact with it. Be it alliances, wanting to gain control of a town to tax, or world domination, all of these ambitions lead to grand stories.visited before, Mount Angel, but was the first time I have gotten to experience the seminary side of it.
